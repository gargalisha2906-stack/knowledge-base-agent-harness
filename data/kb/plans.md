# Plans and Pricing

Starter plan: 29 USD per month, 3 seats, 1,000 tickets per month, email support
with a 2-business-day response time.

Growth plan: 99 USD per month, 15 seats, 10,000 tickets per month, live chat
support and API access with 1,000 requests per minute.

Scale plan: 399 USD per month, unlimited seats and tickets, 99.9% uptime SLA,
a named support engineer, SSO/SAML, and audit logs.

All plans include the knowledge base, ticket routing, and basic analytics.
Yearly billing gives two months free on any plan. Nonprofits and schools get
50% off Growth and Scale with verification. Free trials run 14 days on Growth
features and downgrade to a read-only archive unless a plan is chosen.
