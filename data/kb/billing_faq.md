# Billing FAQ

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and bank transfer for yearly
plans above 500 USD. We do not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

Invoices are emailed automatically after each payment and are also available
under Settings > Billing > Invoices as PDF downloads. GST and VAT are added at
checkout based on the billing country; enter your tax ID under Settings >
Billing > Tax info before payment to have it appear on the invoice.

A failed payment retries automatically three times over 7 days. If all retries
fail, the workspace switches to read-only mode for 14 days before suspension.
Update your card under Settings > Billing > Payment method to trigger a fresh
charge immediately.

Changing plans mid-cycle is prorated: upgrades are charged immediately at the
prorated difference, downgrades apply from the next billing date.
