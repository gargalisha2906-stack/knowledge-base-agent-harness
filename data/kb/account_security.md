# Account and Security

Admins invite teammates from Settings > Team. Invites expire after 7 days.
Roles: Admin (billing + team management), Member (daily work), Viewer
(read-only). Changing a role takes effect immediately.

Enable two-factor authentication under Settings > Security. TOTP authenticator
apps are supported; SMS codes are not, because SIM-swap attacks make SMS the
weaker second factor. Ten backup codes are shown once when 2FA is enabled.

Data export: admins can export all workspace data as JSON from Settings >
Data. Exports include tickets, messages, and attachments. Account deletion is
self-serve from Settings > Data > Delete workspace; deletion is soft for 30
days (restorable by support) and permanent after that.

We never ask for your password by email or chat. Sessions expire after 14 days
of inactivity. Sign-in alerts are emailed for new devices.
