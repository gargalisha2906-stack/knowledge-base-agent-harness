# Northwind Software Refund Policy

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop renewing at the end of the
current billing month. Monthly payments are not refunded for partial months.

Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days,
yearly plans are refunded pro-rata for the remaining full months, minus a 10%
administration fee. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within
5-7 business days.

To request a refund, email billing@northwind.example from the account email
address and include your invoice number. Refund requests that need manual
review: invoices older than 12 months, payments made by bank transfer, and any
refund above 500 USD. Manual review takes up to 3 business days.

Duplicate charges are always refunded in full, regardless of plan or age, once
the duplicate is confirmed by our billing team. Chargebacks opened with the
bank before contacting support pause the account until resolved.
